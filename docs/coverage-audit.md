## Core set

Clinical tests, batteries, mood and validity measures follow the most frequently used instruments in the Rabin et al. surveys. Affective, social and research tasks are not covered by those surveys, so the core set takes the standard tasks used in the lesion and patient studies behind the cases (for example the Iowa Gambling Task, Faux Pas and TASIT). Diagnostic criteria are the current consensus criteria for the conditions in the core cases. Core: 24 cases and 105 instruments; extended: 37 cases and 275 instruments.

- Rabin, L. A., Barr, W. B., & Burton, L. A. (2005). Assessment practices of clinical neuropsychologists in the United States and Canada: A survey of INS, NAN, and APA Division 40 members. Archives of Clinical Neuropsychology, 20(1), 33–65.
- Rabin, L. A., Paolillo, E., & Barr, W. B. (2016). Stability in test-usage practices of clinical neuropsychologists in the United States and Canada over a 10-year period: A follow-up survey of INS and NAN members. Archives of Clinical Neuropsychology, 31(3), 206–230.

- **ERP**, EEG and event-related potential markers (Psychophysiology; Research). Subscales or parts: Error-related negativity (ERN); Feedback-related negativity / reward positivity; P300 (P3b); Late positive potential (emotional pictures); Frontal alpha asymmetry.

- **TMS / tDCS**, Non-invasive brain stimulation (TMS, tDCS) as a virtual lesion (Experimental method; Research). Subscales or parts: Repetitive TMS (inhibitory or excitatory); Theta-burst stimulation; Transcranial direct current stimulation (anodal, cathodal); Sham control.
- **EMA / VR**, Ecological momentary assessment and naturalistic tasks (Research method; Research). Subscales or parts: Experience sampling of mood and fear (as with patient SM); Smartphone and wearable data; Virtual reality errands and social scenarios; Real-world financial records.
- **Facial EMG**, Facial electromyography (Research method; Research). Subscales or parts: Corrugator supercilii (negative affect); Zygomaticus major (positive affect); Levator labii (disgust); Startle eyeblink (orbicularis oculi).

- **iEEG / stimulation**, Intracranial recording and stimulation (Research method; Research). Subscales or parts: Stereo-EEG and electrocorticography; Single-neuron recording; Direct electrical stimulation and the emotions it evokes; Local field potentials from DBS electrodes.
- **Wada**, Intracarotid amobarbital (Wada) test (Research method; Both). Subscales or parts: Language lateralization; Memory lateralization; Emotional reaction after injection.
- **Drug challenge**, Pharmacological challenge and on/off medication designs (Research method; Research). Subscales or parts: On versus off dopaminergic medication; Placebo-controlled single doses (e.g., L-dopa, sulpiride, propranolol, oxytocin); Tryptophan depletion (serotonin).
