The Feeling Brain Library

A case library and assessment reference on the neuropsychology of affective decision-making. Explore how damage to brain systems for value, threat, reward, body states, expression and awareness changes reward learning, decisions and emotional behavior.

Each case takes about 8 to 10 minutes. All patient profiles except Phineas Gage are illustrative composites based on published cases, not real patient records.

We are not thinking machines that feel; we are feeling machines that think.attributed to Antonio Damasio
